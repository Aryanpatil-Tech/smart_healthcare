ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Connected records.
Consent at the center.

CareVault is a development concept for patient-controlled digital health records shared with authorized care providers.

How access works

  1. 1
    Verify the care team

    Doctors and hospitals apply for an account; administrators review credentials before access features are enabled.

  2. 2
    Request only what is needed

    The provider locates a patient using a Patient ID or QR identity token and selects record categories and a purpose.

  3. 3
    The patient decides

    Records stay private until the patient approves. Permission expires automatically and can be revoked early.

ImportantThis software is a prototype. Independent security, privacy, clinical and regulatory review is required before any real-world deployment.
